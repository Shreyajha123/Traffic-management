Project Objective & Academic Scope

Bridging traditional Machine Learning regression with Generative AI urban mobility policy synthesis.

Traffic Data Analytics

Analyzes multi-factor urban variables including temporal spikes, weather severity, and known bottleneck corridors across Bengaluru.

ML Volume Prediction

Implements Scikit-Learn RandomForestRegressor to forecast vehicle volume per hour with full preprocessing pipelines.

Congestion Classification

Categorizes corridor loads into Low (<300), Medium (300-700), and High (>700) tiers to aid municipal priority routing.

Interactive Bengaluru Map

Leaflet.js geospatial visualization rendering 8 critical junctions (Silk Board, Hebbal, KR Puram, etc.) with color-coded status pins.

Gemini AI Assistant

Official Google Gemini SDK conversational assistant answering technical urban mobility queries with offline educational fallbacks.

Autonomous Advisory Agent

Translates ML prediction numbers into 4 actionable policy directives: signal phasing, transit dispatch, and perimeter diversion.

How It Works: Pipeline Architecture

End-to-end artificial intelligence workflow from raw data ingestion to policy synthesis.

1

Synthetic Ingestion

Generates 2,000 parametric records with date, hour, weather, road, and speeds.

2

Feature Engineering

One-hot encoding for categorical corridors and weather + numeric standard scaling.

3

Random Forest ML

Ensemble regression evaluates non-linear temporal interactions and outputs vehicle volume.

4

AI Advisory Agent

Gemini LLM interprets the result and synthesizes 4 tactical traffic mitigation measures.
