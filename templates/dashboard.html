Academic Demonstration Congestion Thresholds
Low Congestion: < 300 vehicles/hr

Free flow traffic, average corridor speeds 40-55 km/h.

Medium Congestion: 300 - 700 vehicles/hr

Moderate density with minor queue delays, speeds 22-38 km/h.

High Congestion: > 700 vehicles/hr

Heavy bumper-to-bumper queueing, speeds 8-18 km/h.

* Note: These numerical thresholds are pedagogical simulation benchmarks established for this FAI mini-project and are not official standards established by the Bengaluru Traffic Police (BTP) or MoRTH.
